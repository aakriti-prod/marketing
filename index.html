All three, in order

One strategist, three jobs, in this order. Most hires cover one. I connect all three, so the brand promise survives all the way to revenue.

01

Step 1: Branding. Decide what the brand stands for.

Before a single post, I answer three questions: who is this for, what do we promise, why should anyone believe us. Positioning, personas, messaging, voice.

What I do different: most teams create content first and wonder why it never converts. I fix the message before the medium, so every asset below sells the same story. Results: B2B reframe, 3 clinic stories, trust-first safety messaging.

Skills: positioning, personas, messaging, voice, audience segmentation, competitive analysis.
Toolkit: Figma, Canva, SEMrush, GA4, SQL.

02

Step 2: Content. Build the engine, then ship.

I turn the brand story into a system that ships: calendars, AI pipelines, pages, guides, video. Then I run it week after week.

What I do different: most writers hand you posts. I hand you an engine: 7 finished assets per brief at 60% lower cost, with me editing every line so it still sounds human. Results: 7 assets per brief, 60+ guides, 200-article KB.

Skills: content strategy, calendars, copywriting, storytelling, lifecycle management, AI refinement, SEO copy.
Toolkit: ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Midjourney, Canva AI, CapCut, Mailchimp, HubSpot, WordPress.

03

Step 3: Marketing. Take it to market and grow it.

I connect content to revenue: GTM plans, launches, funnels, MQL pipeline. Every piece of content has a job: attract, convert, or keep.

What I do different: most content never touches pipeline. Mine is tracked from first click to sales handoff, so marketing spend answers to numbers. Results: $250K pipeline opportunity, +25% engagement.

Skills: GTM, demand gen, MQL-to-SQL, funnel optimization, campaigns, A/B testing, retention, community.
Toolkit: HubSpot, GA4, SEMrush, Tableau, Power BI, SQL, Mailchimp.

Process: Research > Position > Create > Post > Analyze
