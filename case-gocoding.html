Go Coding

Brand & Community Marketing

Impact: Live events turned hundreds of students into active users right away, building a loyal community entirely through word-of-mouth.

Showcase: Free, open-source coding platform fighting for student attention.

Problem: Hard to get busy young coders to notice a new website and actually use it, with zero budget for paid ads.

Why I was onboarded: Zero ad budget, empty classrooms: they needed users without spending.

What I did and how

  • Focused the branding on the best part: open-source and 100% free.
  • Skipped online ads; went out into the community instead.
  • Hosted live, interactive bootcamps and workshops showing students how the platform worked.

Skipped ads entirely. Went to where students already gathered, ran live bootcamps, and let them try the platform on the spot.
