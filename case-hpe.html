HPE Aruba

Product Content & Enablement · 2022

Impact: +17% completion, 20% less onboarding friction, $50K+ new MRR in 90 days, tickets down 23% (800 to 620 a month), +15% trial-to-paid.

Showcase: Enterprise networking giant. Aruba Central: SaaS platform, 10,000+ global users.

Problem: 10,000+ user SaaS platform with weak onboarding completion and high ticket volume; a new analytics feature needed launch content.

Why I was onboarded: Aruba needed onboarding content strong enough to carry a platform relaunch.

What I did and how

  • Led an 8-person team relaunching onboarding with JTBD research.
  • Launched analytics content: positioning, in-app guides, battle cards.
  • Redesigned IA with chatbot docs and visual guides.
  • Lifted completion 17%; promoted in 6 months with GEM recognition.

Started from 800 monthly support tickets: mapped them to JTBD jobs, then rebuilt onboarding around jobs instead of features. Shipped alongside engineers, week by week.
